I am a professor at the **Federal University of Rio Grande (FURG)**, affiliated with the **Center for Computational Sciences (C3)**.

My research explores **organizational multi-agent systems**, **knowledge representation and coordination**, **LLM-augmented distributed intelligence**, and **Digital Twin architectures** for intelligent and adaptive production systems. I seek to connect formal and computational foundations with industrial innovation, international research collaboration, and the education of engineers and researchers.

For a structured overview of my current scientific agenda, see the [Research Program]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}). My academic record is also available through [Lattes](https://lattes.cnpq.br/8560050240967369), [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=iNuF4LMAAAAJ&hl=en), and [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-9159-2264).

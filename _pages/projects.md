My funded project experience combines **foundational research in multi-agent systems** with **industry-oriented technology development**, including collaborations involving FAPERGS, EMBRAPII, FINEP, and Petrobras. The five examples below distinguish projects I **coordinated** from those in which I served as a **team member**.

## Projects I Coordinated

### Planning and Argumentation in Multi-Agent Systems

**Original title:** *Planejamento e Argumentação em Sistemas Multiagentes*  
**Period listed in Lattes:** 2024– (no end year recorded)  
**Funder:** FAPERGS — Rio Grande do Sul Research Support Foundation  
**Role:** **Project Coordinator**  
**Research axis:** Collective Intelligence & Organizational AI

This research investigates how automated planning and argumentation can be combined at the organizational level of multi-agent systems. It explores ways to integrate these capabilities into **JaCaMo**, supporting the analysis and automatic formation of agent groups for complex problems.

### Bio-Pen: Device for Cartilage Processing and Application

**Original title:** *Bio-Caneta: Dispositivo Inovador para Trituração e Aplicação de Cartilagem com Validação Bio-Tecnológica*  
**Period:** 2024–2025  
**Funder:** EMBRAPII — Brazilian Company for Industrial Research and Innovation  
**Role:** **Project Coordinator**  
**Research axis:** Translational AI Systems — Health-Tech and Technology Development

An applied development project focused on a prototype device for controlled cartilage processing and application. The project description specifies dimensional/volumetric control, sensor-supported monitoring, and validation with ex-vivo porcine cartilage. Its planned validation and technology readiness objectives should not be interpreted as independently verified outcomes.

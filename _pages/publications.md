## Organizational Multi-Agent Systems and Formal Methods

### A formal testing method for multi-agent systems using colored Petri nets (2025)

**R. A. Machado, A. S. Z. Cardoso, G. P. Farias, E. M. N. Gonçalves, and D. F. Adamatti.** *Autonomous Agents and Multi-Agent Systems*, 39 (2025).  
[DOI: 10.1007/s10458-025-09690-z](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10458-025-09690-z)

A formal testing approach for multi-agent systems using colored Petri nets, contributing to systematic verification and testing of agent behavior and interactions.

**Research axis:** Collective Intelligence & Organizational AI.

### CPN4M: Testing Multi-Agent Systems under Organizational Model Moise+ using Colored Petri Nets (2022)

**E. M. N. Gonçalves, R. A. Machado, B. C. Rodrigues, and D. Adamatti.** *Applied Sciences*, 12 (2022).  
[DOI: 10.3390/app12125857](https://doi.org/10.3390/app12125857)

Investigates the use of colored Petri nets to support testing of multi-agent systems designed using the Moise+ organizational model.

**Research axis:** Collective Intelligence & Organizational AI.

### Specifying Knowledge in Cognitive Multiagent Systems Using a Class of Hierarchical Petri Nets (2012)

**E. M. N. Gonçalves.** *Journal of Software*, 7 (2012).  
[DOI: 10.4304/jsw.7.11.2405-2414](https://doi.org/10.4304/jsw.7.11.2405-2414)

Explores hierarchical Petri nets as a formal representation for specifying knowledge in cognitive multi-agent systems.

**Research axis:** Collective Intelligence & Organizational AI.

### Model for Knowledge Transfer in Agent Organizations: a Case Study on Moise+ (2024)

**E. M. N. Gonçalves, D. Adamatti, P. Rodrigues, and G. P. Farias.** *The International FLAIRS Conference Proceedings*, 37 (2024).  
[DOI: 10.32473/flairs.37](https://doi.org/10.32473/flairs.37)

Examines knowledge transfer at the agent-organization level using Moise+ as a case study, supporting an emerging research agenda on organizational knowledge in distributed AI.

**Research axis:** Collective Intelligence & Organizational AI.

### MATE: An Automated Organization-Driven Testing Environment for JaCaMo Multi-Agent Systems (2026)

**E. M. N. Gonçalves, G. V. A. Avila, R. A. Machado, and D. Adamatti.** *Proceedings of the XX Workshop-Escola de Sistemas de Agentes, seus Ambientes e Aplicações (WESAAC)* (2026).  
[DOI: 10.5753/wesaac.2026.30220](https://doi.org/10.5753/wesaac.2026.30220)

Presents a testing environment for JaCaMo multi-agent systems, connecting organizational specifications with automated testing support.

**Research axes:** Collective Intelligence & Organizational AI; related enabling methods for distributed AI architectures.

## Digital Twins and Industrial AI

### An AutomationML Based Ontology for Sensor Fusion in Industrial Plants (2019)

**E. Gonçalves, A. Freitas, and S. Botelho.** *Sensors*, 19 (2019).  
[DOI: 10.3390/s19061311](https://doi.org/10.3390/s19061311)

Develops an ontology-based approach using AutomationML to represent and integrate industrial sensing information, relevant to semantic interoperability and industrial digitalization.

**Research axis:** AI-Driven Digital Twins for Adaptive Production.

### Digital Twin Across Industry 5.0: Integrating Dimensional Analysis to a Rotor Inspection Module (2024)

**B. D. Oliveira et al. (including E. M. N. Gonçalves).** *2024 IEEE 22nd International Conference on Industrial Informatics (INDIN)* (2024).  
[DOI: 10.1109/INDIN58382.2024.10774450](https://doi.org/10.1109/INDIN58382.2024.10774450)

Addresses the integration of dimensional analysis into a rotor-inspection Digital Twin, illustrating intelligent inspection workflows in industrial production.

**Research axis:** AI-Driven Digital Twins for Adaptive Production.

### Performance-watt analysis of GPU-based digital twin simulations (2024)

**J. dos Santos, M. Bicho, T. Froes, G. Dorneles, M. Pias, E. Gonçalves, and S. Botelho.** *IECON 2024 — 50th Annual Conference of the IEEE Industrial Electronics Society* (2024).  
[DOI: 10.1109/IECON55916.2024.10983989](https://doi.org/10.1109/IECON55916.2024.10983989)

Studies the performance–power trade-off of GPU-based simulation, an important consideration for efficient Digital Twin implementations.

**Research axis:** AI-Driven Digital Twins for Adaptive Production.

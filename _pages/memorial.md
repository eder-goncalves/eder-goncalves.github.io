# O Tempo Passa e nem Tudo Fica — A Obra Inteira de Uma Vida

*Academic Memorial · Federal University of Rio Grande (FURG), Brazil · October 2026*

This academic memorial was prepared in support of my application for promotion to Full Professor (*Professor Titular*) at the Federal University of Rio Grande (FURG).

Rather than simply offering a chronological account of professional achievements, the memorial presents a personal and critical reflection on my academic trajectory and on the role of the university in society. It connects experiences in **teaching**, **research**, **university extension and technological innovation**, and **academic administration** with broader discussions about engineering education, scientific knowledge, regional development, and institutional responsibility.

The narrative is also autobiographical: music and personal memories provide a distinctive thread through the account. The document preserves this original perspective and is available in **Portuguese**.

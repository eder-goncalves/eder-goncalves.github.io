My teaching and research supervision at the **Federal University of Rio Grande (FURG)** connect multi-agent systems, intelligent manufacturing, robotics, and artificial intelligence. I work with undergraduate students as well as graduate researchers, linking theoretical foundations to applied problems in distributed and cyber-physical systems.

## Advising — Research Supervision

I supervise and co-supervise research projects at undergraduate and graduate levels, including scientific initiation, capstone projects, master's dissertations, and doctoral research. My main supervision areas include:

### Organizational Multi-Agent Systems

Organization-oriented modeling, Moise+, planning and coordination, organizational knowledge transfer, and formal testing and verification of multi-agent systems.

### LLM-Enhanced Intelligent Systems

Language-model-based multi-agent architectures, intelligent recommendation systems, knowledge graphs, retrieval-augmented generation, and semantic processing of technical knowledge.

### Digital Twins and Cyber-Physical Systems

Digital twins for industrial applications, simulation-integrated decision support, inspection processes, and interfaces for complex equipment operations.

### Intelligent Manufacturing and Industrial AI

Distributed manufacturing, industrial automation, production logistics, process optimization, energy efficiency, and intelligent industrial information systems.

### Robotics and Autonomous Systems

Autonomous robotic architectures, robot learning, intelligent coordination, and continuous learning for robotic systems.
